1. EXECUTIVE OVERVIEW & MISSION STATEMENT
================================================================================

SUIT (Court Intelligence & Legal Advocacy Platform) is an enterprise-grade,
AI-powered litigation intelligence, judicial analytics, and procedural drafting
platform engineered specifically for the Indian Legal System.

The Indian judiciary represents one of the largest and most complex legal ecosystems
in the world. Governed by Article 141 of the Constitution of India (whereby law
declared by the Supreme Court of India is binding on all subordinate courts), it
encompasses over 50 million pending cases across the Supreme Court of India, 25 High
Courts, and thousands of District and Subordinate Courts. Practitioners face
enormous informational friction: scattered eCourts cause lists, unindexed PDF orders,
intricate binding precedent hierarchies, and the monumental July 2024 legislative
overhaul of India's foundational criminal codes.

SUIT is designed from the ground up to solve these systemic challenges. It bridges
the chasm between raw judicial databases and real-world courtroom advocacy.
By uniting specialized domain transformers (law-ai/InLegalBERT), state-of-the-art
generative reasoning models (Google Gemini 3.1 Flash-Lite, OpenRouter, Groq),
deterministic statutory rule engines, and privacy-first local offline models
(Ollama Qwen 2.5 7B), SUIT transforms raw dockets into:
  1. Actionable judicial outcome predictions based on empirical precedent analogy.
  2. Publisher-grade headnotes with hierarchical catchwords and operative ratio decidendi.
  3. Seamless criminal law era transitions (IPC/CrPC/IEA <-> BNS/BNSS/BSA).
  4. Court-ready adversarial defense pleadings (Order VIII CPC Written Statements).
  5. Registry-compliant paper-book drafting with interactive procedural deficit audits.

2. WHY SUIT IS NEEDED (COMPREHENSIVE PAIN POINT & INDUSTRY ANALYSIS)
================================================================================

2.1 PAIN POINT 1: DOCKET TRACKING & DATA FRAGMENTATION
  - Context: The eCourts portal houses case details across 3,000+ court complexes.
    Lawyers managing 50-100 active matters must manually input 16-character Case
    Number Record (CNR) strings repeatedly, solve CAPTCHAs, and navigate cryptic
    cause lists.
  - The SUIT Solution: Single-click CNR lookup, persistent caching, automated
    timeline generation with staggered hearing date extrapolation, and one-click
    case refreshing.

2.2 PAIN POINT 2: COMPREHENDING 100-PAGE JUDGMENTS UNDER TIME PRESSURE
  - Context: An advocate appearing before a High Court bench often has under 5
    minutes to distill a 70-page judgment cited unexpectedly by opposing counsel.
    Judgments intermingle counsel submissions, trial court factual records, obiter
    dicta, and operative holdings.
  - The SUIT Solution: The Headnote & Order Intelligence Engine performs Rhetorical
    Role Segmentation (RRS) using InLegalBERT to mathematically isolate the ratio
    decidendi from party arguments, producing SCC/AIR-style publisher headnotes with
    hierarchical catchwords, numbered held points, and plain-language summaries.

2.3 PAIN POINT 3: THE JULY 2024 STATUTORY TRANSITION CRISIS
  - Context: On 1 July 2024, the Parliament of India repealed and replaced the three
    foundational criminal law codes:
      1. Indian Penal Code, 1860 (IPC) -> Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, 2023 (BNS)
      2. Code of Criminal Procedure, 1973 (CrPC) -> Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita, 2023 (BNSS)
      3. Indian Evidence Act, 1872 (IEA) -> Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam, 2023 (BSA)
    Under Section 531 BNSS and Section 358 BNS, offences committed prior to 1 July 2024
    must be investigated and tried under legacy codes, while offences committed thereafter
    fall strictly under BNS/BNSS. Thousands of pleadings are dismissed or returned
    with registry objections due to incorrect sections or failure to comply with
    new procedural mandates (e.g., mandatory videography under S. 105 BNSS).
  - The SUIT Solution: The Criminal Era Transition Suite features:
    * Bidirectional Concordance Lookup (e.g. IPC 420 <-> BNS 318(4)).
    * Verbatim Word-Level Statutory Diff Engine (highlighting additions, deletions, penalty deltas).
    * Temporal Safeguard Engine (verifying incident date vs Article 20(1) ex post facto bars).
    * Transitional Jurisprudence Feed (curated high court rulings interpreting S. 531 BNSS).
    * Court-Ready Pleading Arguments based on Section 8 General Clauses Act, 1897.

2.4 PAIN POINT 4: PRECEDENT SEARCH & THE BINDING AUTHORITY HIERARCHY
  - Context: Finding a precedent that is "on all fours" requires evaluating judicial
    hierarchy (Supreme Court 3-judge bench > 2-judge bench > High Court full bench >
    High Court division bench). Generic search engines fail to distinguish whether a
    case was followed, distinguished, or overruled.
  - The SUIT Solution: Hybrid RAG fuses Kanoon citation graphs with InLegalBERT dense
    embeddings, applies a 1.5x stare decisis multiplier for Supreme Court judgments,
    and constructs an interactive D3 citation network graph with "Why This Helps" strategic badges.

2.5 PAIN POINT 5: DRUDGERY OF OPPOSING PLEADING DRAFTING (ORDER VIII CPC)
  - Context: When served with a 40-page Plaint or Writ Petition, advocates must draft
    a Written Statement adhering to Order VIII Rules 3-5 CPC (mandating that every
    allegation not specifically denied is deemed to be admitted). Drafting 50+
    paragraph-by-paragraph denials and identifying legal bars consumes 6-10 hours.
  - The SUIT Solution: The Adversarial Defense Engine ingests the plaint, extracts
    numbered paragraphs via deterministic regex, flags statutory bars (Order VII
    Rule 11, Section 12A Commercial Courts Act, Limitation Act), and drafts a
    complete Written Statement ready for insertion into the built-in Legal Editor.

2.6 PAIN POINT 6: REGISTRY SCRUTINY REJECTIONS & DEFECTIVE PAPER-BOOKS
  - Context: High Court and Supreme Court registries reject up to 30% of newly filed
    petitions due to technical procedural deficits: missing verification affidavits,
    failure to plead territorial jurisdiction, omitted limitation averments, or
    incorrect line spacing and left-hand gutter margins.
  - The SUIT Solution: The Desktop-Exclusive Legal Drafting Studio incorporates the
    Procedural Deficit Guard (deterministic statutory rule validation) and generates
    court-ready DOCX paper-books adhering to High Court filing rules (1.5" left margin,
    1" right margin, double line spacing).

4. SYSTEM ARCHITECTURE & DESIGN PHILOSOPHY
================================================================================

SUIT is engineered with a modern, high-performance, two-tier decoupled architecture:
  1. Frontend: Single Page Application (SPA) built on React 19, TypeScript, and Vite.
  2. Backend: Asynchronous REST API built on Python 3.12/3.14, FastAPI, and Uvicorn.

CORE ARCHITECTURAL PRINCIPLES:

  1. Local-First AI & Dual-Mode Flexibility:
     - Cloud Mode: Uses Google Gemini 3.1 Flash-Lite for high-speed structured extraction,
       OpenRouter for SSE streaming chat, and Groq Cloud as an automatic high-speed fallback.
     - Local Mode: Uses Ollama (Qwen 2.5 7B) running entirely on localhost:11434.
       Zero bytes of sensitive case text leave the lawyer's machine, satisfying Section 126
       Indian Evidence Act (and Section 132 BSA) advocate-client privilege.
     - Automatic Failover: When cloud API quotas (HTTP 429) are exhausted, the
       system prompts the user to switch seamlessly to the local model.

  2. Neuro-Symbolic Legal Processing:
     - Combines symbolic deterministic rules (regex parsing, statutory bar logic,
       verbatim diffs, concordance tables) with neural AI models (InLegalBERT embeddings,
       Gemini generative deduction). This guarantees 100% legal accuracy on
       statutory section numbers while leveraging LLM reasoning for prose synthesis.

  3. Two-Tier Caching with Graceful Degradation:
     - Tier 1: In-memory Redis cache for high-frequency search and case queries.
     - Tier 2: Relational persistence (SQLite in dev, PostgreSQL in prod) for
       cached orders, headnotes, predictions, and analysis. If Redis is down,
       the system transparently falls back to the database without throwing errors.

  4. Desktop-First Procedural Advocacy:
     - Intensive legal drafting, multi-page paper-book editing, and defense war room
       analytics are optimized for desktop viewports (>= 1024px). Mobile devices are
       served with responsive docket viewers, cause lists, and quick search.

6.4 DATABASE LAYER & ASYNCHRONOUS SESSION MANAGEMENT (backend/app/database/)
----------------------------------------------------------------------------
SUIT implements an asynchronous database access layer using SQLAlchemy 2.0:

SQLite Configuration (Development):
  - Driver: `aiosqlite`
  - Pool: `StaticPool` - ensures all async tasks within a process reuse the single
    underlying connection, eliminating "database is locked" errors in SQLite.
  - Connection PRAGMAs:
    * `PRAGMA journal_mode=WAL`: Enables Write-Ahead Logging, allowing concurrent
      readers alongside a single writer.
    * `PRAGMA busy_timeout=30000`: Sets a 30-second lock retry timeout.
    * `PRAGMA foreign_keys=ON`: Enforces relational integrity on delete/update.

PostgreSQL Configuration (Production):
  - Driver: `asyncpg`
  - Pool: `NullPool` (stateless connection pool suitable for serverless or container
    scaling) or `QueuePool` with `pool_size=10, max_overflow=20`.
  - `pool_pre_ping=True`: Verifies connection liveness before issuing queries.

6.5 MIDDLEWARE STACK (LIFO EXECUTION CHAIN)
-------------------------------------------
1. `AIProviderMiddleware`:
   - Inspects the `X-AI-Provider` HTTP header on inbound requests.
   - Sets `request.state.ai_provider = "ollama"` or `"cloud"`.
2. `RateLimitMiddleware`:
   - Distributed token-bucket algorithm using Redis (`ratelimit:{client_ip}`).
   - Default: 120 requests per minute with graceful degradation if Redis is offline.
3. `RequestLoggingMiddleware`:
   - Structured JSON logging via structlog. Records elapsed time in milliseconds (`elapsed_ms`).
4. `CORSMiddleware`:
   - Configured with regex allowing localhost, vercel.app, and duckdns.org origins.
   - Exposes `Content-Disposition` and `X-AI-Provider` headers.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------

Request Interceptor:

  - Reads `useAIModeStore.getEffectiveProvider()`. If `'local'`, injects `X-AI-Provider: ollama`.

Response Interceptor (State Machine):
  - Mutexed Token Refresh (HTTP 401):
    If an access token expires:
    1. Queues concurrent requests.
    2. Issues `POST /api/auth/refresh` using the refresh token.
    3. Replays queued requests with the new token.
    4. If refresh fails, purges tokens and dispatches `auth:logout`.
  - Online AI Limit Interception (HTTP 429 / `ONLINE_AI_LIMIT_REACHED`):
    1. Captures failed request closure in `pendingRetry`.
    2. Opens `SwitchToOllamaModal`.
    3. Upon user confirmation: sets AI mode to `'local'` and retries the request seamlessly.

9.2 LAW-AI/INLEGALBERT - DOMAIN-SPECIFIC LEGAL NLP ENGINE
---------------------------------------------------------
Generic BERT models suffer from representation collapse (anisotropy) and lack comprehension

Indian Supreme Court and High Court judgments.

Key Capabilities:
  - Understands Indian legal terminology: *ratio decidendi*, *res judicata*, *locus standi*,

  - Dense precedent analogy scoring and semantic re-ranking.

9.3 MATHEMATICAL FORMULATIONS
-----------------------------

1. Second-Order SVD Whitening Transformation for Legal Embeddings:
   Raw BERT embeddings often occupy a narrow cone in vector space. To restore isotropy,
   InLegalBERT embeddings undergo second-order whitening:

     v_whitened = (v_raw - mu) * W

   Where:
     - mu is the precomputed mean vector (dimension: 768).
     - W is the whitening transformation matrix (dimension: 768 x 768) derived from
       Singular Value Decomposition (SVD) of the covariance matrix:
       Sigma = U * Lambda * U^T  =>  W = U * Lambda^(-1/2).
   Whitened vectors are L2-normalized:
     
     v_final = v_whitened / ||v_whitened||_2

   Cosine similarity on whitened vectors corresponds to exact Euclidean geometry on
   a unit hypersphere.

2. Reciprocal Rank Fusion (RRF) with Stare Decisis Authority Multiplier:
   When ranking precedents, the Hybrid RAG engine merges rank lists from Citation
   Graph (r_cit), Dense Vector Search (r_vec), and InLegalBERT (r_bert):

     RRF_Score(d) = Alpha(d) * SUM_{m in M} [ 1 / (k + r_m(d)) ]

   Where:
     - k is a smoothing constant (default: 60).
     - M = {CitationGraph, DenseVector, InLegalBERT}.
     - Alpha(d) is the Stare Decisis Multiplier:
       * Alpha = 1.5 if d is a Supreme Court of India judgment (Article 141 binding law).
       * Alpha = 1.2 if d is a High Court judgment.
       * Alpha = 1.0 for Subordinate Court orders.

9.4 RHETORICAL ROLE SEGMENTATION (RRS) & ANCHOR CENTROIDS
---------------------------------------------------------
Headnote extraction employs anchor-based Rhetorical Role Segmentation:
  - Holding Anchors (`_RATIO_ANCHORS`): "We hold that...", "The question of law is answered
    in the affirmative...", "In view of the settled position...", "Accordingly, the appeal is allowed..."
  - Argument Anchors (`_ARGUMENT_ANCHORS`): "The learned counsel contended...", "It was
    submitted on behalf of the appellant...", "The respondent argued that..."
  - Each judgment sentence is embedded via InLegalBERT and projected against the
    anchor centroids. Sentences with minimum cosine distance to `_ARGUMENT_ANCHORS` are
    pruned, preventing counsel arguments from being misidentified as court holdings.

9.5 ERA STATUTORY GUIDE & PROMPT ENFORCEMENTS
---------------------------------------------
All generative prompts for prediction, headnote synthesis, and drafting inject the
`ERA_STATUTORY_GUIDE` system directive:
  - If filing date >= 2024-07-01: Prohibits citing S. 439 CrPC (must cite S. 483 BNSS),
    S. 438 CrPC (must cite S. 482 BNSS), S. 482 CrPC (must cite S. 528 BNSS),
    S. 420 IPC (must cite S. 318(4) BNS), S. 65B Evidence Act (must cite S. 63 BSA).
  - If filing date < 2024-07-01: Enforces legacy statutory references with Section 8

12. AUTHENTICATION, AUTHORIZATION & SECURITY ARCHITECTURE
================================================================================

12.1 EMAIL/PASSWORD REGISTRATION & BREVO/SMTP OTP VERIFICATION FLOW
------------------------------------------------------------------
  1. Registration (`POST /api/auth/register`):
     - Validates email syntax and hashes password using `passlib` bcrypt (12 rounds).
     - Inserts unverified user record (`is_verified=False`).
     - Generates cryptographically secure 6-digit numeric OTP (`secrets.randbelow(900000) + 100000`).
     - Hashes OTP with SHA-256 and stores in `email_verification` table (10-minute TTL).
     - Dispatches HTML verification email via Brevo API / SMTP server.
  2. OTP Verification (`POST /api/auth/verify-otp`):
     - Receives `email` and plain `otp_code`.
     - Hashes submitted OTP and matches against unexpired, unused record.
     - Sets `used=True` and updates `users.is_verified=True`.
     - Generates paired JWT access and refresh tokens.

12.2 GOOGLE OAUTH2 CREDENTIAL VERIFICATION FLOW
-----------------------------------------------
  1. Frontend SDK (`@react-oauth/google`) renders Google Sign-In button and receives ID token.
  2. Backend Verification (`POST /api/auth/google-auth`):
     - Validates ID token via `google.oauth2.id_token.verify_oauth2_token`.
     - Extracts verified email, full name, Google user ID (`sub`), and picture.
     - Upserts user record with `auth_provider='google'` and `is_verified=True`.
     - Returns SUIT JWT token pair.

12.3 DUAL-SECRET JWT ARCHITECTURE & TOKEN REFRESH FLOW
------------------------------------------------------
  - Access Token:
    * Signed with `JWT_SECRET` using `HS256`.
    * Payload: `{ sub: user_id, email: email, exp: now + 30m, type: 'access' }`.
  - Refresh Token:
    * Signed with `JWT_REFRESH_SECRET` using `HS256`.
    * Payload: `{ sub: user_id, exp: now + 7d, type: 'refresh' }`.
  - Token Refresh Endpoint (`POST /api/auth/refresh`):
    * Verifies `type == 'refresh'`.
    * Issues new access token and rotatable refresh token.

12.4 DATA CONFIDENTIALITY, ADVOCATE-CLIENT PRIVILEGE & AIR-GAPPED PRIVACY
-------------------------------------------------------------------------
Under Section 126 of the Indian Evidence Act, 1872 (and Section 132 of the Bharatiya
Sakshya Adhiniyam, 2023), communications between an advocate and client are privileged.
  - Air-Gapped Local AI Execution:
    Lawyers handling sensitive criminal, commercial, or matrimonial matters can toggle
    AI Mode to "Local" (Ollama Qwen 2.5 7B). All paragraph traversals, draft audits,
    and chat queries execute entirely on `localhost:11434`. Zero prompt tokens leave the machine.
  - Ephemeral Drafting Memory:
    Unsaved text in the Desktop Legal Studio or Adversarial Defense Engine remains in
    client browser memory until explicitly saved by the user.

17.1 LOCAL DEVELOPMENT WORKFLOW
  - Prerequisites: Python 3.12+, Node.js 20+, Docker (for Redis).
  - Launching:
    ```powershell
    # Run the automated launch script
    .\start-all.ps1
    ```
    Concurrently boots:
    1. FastAPI Backend on `http://localhost:8000` via Uvicorn reload.
    2. React Frontend on `http://localhost:5173` via Vite dev server.
    3. Redis 7 container on port 6379 via `docker-compose up -d`.

17.2 PRODUCTION ARCHITECTURE ON AWS EC2 & DUCKDNS
  - Live Host: AWS EC2 Ubuntu 24.04 LTS instance.
  - Domain: `https://suit-tc.duckdns.org`
  - Reverse Proxy (Nginx): Terminates SSL/TLS (Let's Encrypt Certbot), routes `/api/*`
    to Uvicorn ASGI on port 8000, and serves static frontend builds with aggressive
    gzip/brotli compression.

17.3 NGINX CONFIGURATION SNIPPET
  ```nginx
  server {
      server_name suit-tc.duckdns.org;

      location /api/ {
          proxy_pass http://127.0.0.1:8000;
          proxy_set_header Host $host;
          proxy_set_header X-Real-IP $remote_addr;
          proxy_set_header X-Forwarded-For $proxy_add_x_forwarded_for;
          proxy_set_header X-Forwarded-Proto $scheme;
          proxy_buffering off;
          proxy_read_timeout 300s;
      }

      location / {
          root /var/www/suit/frontend/dist;
          index index.html;
          try_files $uri $uri/ /index.html;
      }

      listen 443 ssl;
      ssl_certificate /etc/letsencrypt/live/suit-tc.duckdns.org/fullchain.pem;
      ssl_certificate_key /etc/letsencrypt/live/suit-tc.duckdns.org/privkey.pem;
  }
  ```

17.4 SYSTEMD SERVICE UNITS
  - Backend Service (`suit-backend.service`):
    Runs `uvicorn app.main:app --host 0.0.0.0 --port 8000 --workers 2` inside virtualenv.
  - Automatically restarts on failure (`Restart=always`).

17.5 HORIZONTAL SCALING & RESOURCE LIMITS
  - Stateless Web Tier: FastAPI backend instances do not maintain in-process session
    state. They scale horizontally behind load balancers.
  - InLegalBERT Memory Footprint: The PyTorch InLegalBERT model runs in-process,
    consuming ~450MB of RAM per worker with CPU threads capped at 2 to prevent starvation.

18. TROUBLESHOOTING & OPERATIONAL RUNBOOK
================================================================================

1. Problem: "database is locked" error in development.
   - Cause: SQLite does not support concurrent write transactions across multiple processes.
   - Fix: Ensure `DATABASE_URL` uses `sqlite+aiosqlite:///./suits.db` with `StaticPool`
     and `PRAGMA journal_mode=WAL` (configured automatically in `session.py`).

2. Problem: Cloud AI calls fail with HTTP 429 (`ONLINE_AI_LIMIT_REACHED`).
   - Cause: Gemini or OpenRouter API rate limits or daily quotas exhausted.
   - Automatic Mitigation: The frontend automatically displays `SwitchToOllamaModal`.
     Clicking "Switch to Local AI" redirects inference to the local Ollama daemon.

3. Problem: Ollama local model reports "Model not found".
   - Fix: Ensure Ollama is running (`ollama serve`) and pull the required model:
     ```bash
     ollama run qwen2.5:7b
     ```

4. Problem: Scanned PDF extraction returns empty text.
   - Fix: Verify `GEMINI_API_KEY` is configured in `backend/.env`. Scanned orders are
     automatically routed to Gemini Multimodal Vision OCR.

5. Problem: Desktop header renders twice on wide screens.
   - Fix: Resolved in v2.0.0. `MobileHeader` is isolated to `< 768px` (`md:hidden`),
     while wide desktop screens render only `TopSearchBar` and collapsible `Sidebar`.

### 4. AI Stack & External Integration Clients

Located in `backend/app/clients/`:

1. **`kanoon_client.py` (Indian Kanoon API)**:
   - Full-text judgment retrieval, metadata, citations (`cites`/`citedby`), and official PDF streaming.
2. **`gemini_client.py` (Google Gemini API)**:
   - Google Gemini 3.1 Flash-Lite / 2.5 Flash for multimodal court PDF vision OCR, dense embeddings, and structured legal JSON extraction.
3. **`prediction_gemini_client.py` (High-Thinking Prediction Client)**:
   - Dedicated Gemini client with 24,576 thinking tokens for judicial outcome probability deduction and ratio synthesis.
4. **`openrouter_client.py` (OpenRouter API)**:
   - Low-latency conversational legal chat with Server-Sent Events (SSE) token streaming.
5. **`groq_client.py` (Groq Cloud API)**:
   - High-throughput cloud fallback (`llama-3.3-70b-versatile` / `openai/gpt-oss-120b`) when Gemini rate limits are hit.
6. **`ollama_client.py` (Local Ollama Daemon)**:
   - Connects to `localhost:11434` for 100% offline, confidential drafting using `qwen2.5:7b`.
7. **`ecourts_client.py` (eCourts Partner API)**:
   - 2-second fast-fail client for retrieving live court dockets, cause lists, and hearing histories.
